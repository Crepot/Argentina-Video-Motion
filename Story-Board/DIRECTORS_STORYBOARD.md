# ARGENTINA — A HISTORY DRAWN IN LINES

## Director's storyboard · Design phase

**Format:** 16:9 · 1920 × 1080 reference canvas  
**Runtime:** 01:45 exactly · 19 scenes · 30 fps reference timing (3,150 frames)  
**Language on screen:** Spanish only  
**Core device:** one continuous **memory line** travels through the atlas. It begins as an Atlantic route, becomes a colonial connection, a revolutionary pulse, a military and migrant route, a railway, a broken timeline, a football trajectory, and finally the outline of Argentina.

The supplied reference image is the visual north star for palette, paper, linework, and editorial scale—not a shot list. The film must feel less like 16 illustrated panels and more like one enormous sheet of cartography under a moving camera. No scene fully clears the page. At least one line, grid, contour, coordinate, or texture survives every transition.

### Dramatic arc

1. **A world draws itself** — distant, exploratory, pre-national.
2. **The line becomes a nation** — rupture, independence, fragmentation, union.
3. **The nation becomes a network** — migration, rail, city, mass politics.
4. **The network is wounded** — dictatorship, compromised celebration, war.
5. **A line of memory is completed** — 1986 to 2022.
6. **All lines become one geography** — contemporary Argentina and `LIBERTAD`.

### Continuity rules

- Hard cuts are reserved for two impacts only: **25 MAY 1810** and the third **LIBERTAD**. Everything else is a path-follow, map morph, match shape, or camera move.
- The memory line is always identifiable by a slightly warmer sky blue and a fine gold core only at earned milestones.
- Text belongs to the map: labels align to coordinates, contours, façades, or timelines. It never sits inside floating cards.
- The visible frame is usually only a crop of a much larger atlas. Camera movement reveals that historical periods were already spatially connected.
- Gold has a narrative budget: trace in 1810, first clear statement in 1816, restrained thereafter, compromised in 1978, incomplete in 1986/2014, completed in 2022, maximal only on the final `LIBERTAD`.

## Complete scene-by-scene storyboard

## 01 — The world before Argentina

**Timestamp / duration:** `00:00–00:06` · **6 s**  
**Historical period:** `1492–1776`

**On-screen text:**

> 1492  
> EL MUNDO SE CONECTA

then

> 1776  
> VIRREINATO DEL RÍO DE LA PLATA

**Anthem:** None.

**Starting composition:** An almost blank ivory sheet. A compass tick appears at upper left; latitude and longitude lines quietly draw outward until a simplified Atlantic planisphere becomes legible. Europe is small at upper right, the Americas at left/center. No modern Argentine border is present.

**Animation / action:** A single sky-blue maritime route leaves Iberia, arcs west, branches into several pale Atlantic traces, then bends south along the coast. Tiny engraved ship silhouettes are constructed from 8–12 strokes as the route passes. `1492` appears once, then recedes into the map as the camera moves. At the Río de la Plata, administrative regions of the 1776 viceroyalty reveal through a translucent period-appropriate wash; `1776` anchors beside the estuary.

**Camera:** Slow oblique push across the Atlantic, followed by a gentle clockwise rotation and a faster final descent toward southern South America. Depth comes from three parallax layers: paper/grid, coasts, routes/labels.

**Color treatment:** Warm ivory base; pale sky-blue water hatching; deep-blue coast and type; the colonial region uses a muted sand/sky-blue crosshatch, not the modern national blue fill.

**Gold:** One hairline compass needle only—orientation, not achievement.

**Transition:** The viceroyalty's eastern boundary continues beyond frame as the same line becomes a wave crest in the Río de la Plata. Ships enter along it.

**Musical energy:** **ATMOSPHERIC → BUILDING**, intensity 1→2.

**Suggested SFX:** Distant ocean, restrained paper scratch, one low ship-timber creak.

**Remotion / SVG feasibility:** Layered SVG planisphere; coast/path reveals via `stroke-dasharray`; ships as reusable symbol instances; labels scale inversely to camera zoom. Historical territory must be a dedicated simplified path, not a recolored modern Argentina polygon.

## 02 — The connection weakens

**Timestamp / duration:** `00:06–00:11` · **5 s**  
**Historical period:** `1806–1808`

**On-screen text:**

> 1806–1807  
> INVASIONES INGLESAS

then

> 1808  
> ESPAÑA EN CRISIS

**Anthem:**

> OÍD, MORTALES…

**Starting composition:** The Río de la Plata fills the lower half; Buenos Aires is a small engraved shoreline cluster. Three ship silhouettes approach on converging navy routes.

**Animation / action:** The approaching routes press against the shoreline, then rebound into civic counter-routes from the city. Rather than depicting battle, route arrows knot and reverse. The camera catches the colonial connection line and travels back across the Atlantic. Near Spain, a minimal bicorne silhouette and angular military arrows signal Napoleonic pressure. The Spain–Río de la Plata line loses segments, as though ink no longer reaches the paper.

**Camera:** Fast route-follow eastward across the ocean, decelerating over Iberia; a slight 8° tilt makes the world feel unstable.

**Color treatment:** Navy and desaturated sky blue; sand-gray Europe; ivory remains luminous. The disrupted imperial connection fades toward pale gray-blue.

**Gold:** None.

**Transition:** A gap in the imperial line expands into negative space. Its two broken ends rotate to become the side walls of the Cabildo façade.

**Musical energy:** **BUILDING → TENSE**, intensity 2→4.

**Suggested SFX:** Wind in rigging, distant cannon-like low thud kept abstract, taut line snap, then a held breath.

**Remotion / SVG feasibility:** One world-space route drives both camera position and trim-path animation. Segment disappearance uses a mask with irregular but deterministic edges. Napoleon is a 10–15-path silhouette, never a portrait.

## 03 — The first impact

**Timestamp / duration:** `00:11–00:17` · **6 s**  
**Historical year:** `1810`

**On-screen text:**

> 25 MAY 1810  
> REVOLUCIÓN DE MAYO

**Anthem:**

> …EL GRITO SAGRADO

**Starting composition:** The broken-line geometry finishes drawing the Cabildo in fine deep-blue architectural strokes. It sits frontal but slightly cropped; the plaza is mostly empty ivory.

**Animation / action:** Rain-like vertical hatching falls. Small umbrella and crowd silhouettes accumulate from isolated dots. At the first major musical impact, a sky-blue pulse moves through the crowd, rises up the Cabildo axis, and replaces muted colonial route color with Argentine sky blue. `25 MAY 1810` lands large; the event line appears smaller beneath it. The phrase `…EL GRITO SAGRADO` reveals from the center outward as though carried by the crowd.

**Camera:** A controlled dolly toward the Cabildo clock/central arch, then a short pullback on impact to reveal the full crowd—the film's first deliberate spatial shock.

**Color treatment:** Ivory, deep blue, and a decisive expansion of sky blue. No red partisan coding and no literal flag tableau.

**Gold:** A 6–8 frame glint on the Cabildo axis, the first hint that some dates will be memorialized.

**Transition:** The sky-blue civic pulse exits the Cabildo as a ribbon. The ribbon crosses a map contour and becomes Belgrano's flag route northward.

**Musical energy:** **EPIC / FIRST IMPACT**, intensity 4→7, then 5.

**Suggested SFX:** Rain texture, restrained crowd inhale/cheer, one architectural bass impact, fabric unfurl.

**Remotion / SVG feasibility:** Façade as grouped symmetric SVG paths with a center-out reveal; crowd generated from a curated library of 12 silhouettes with seeded placement; typography attached to the building's screen-space anchor so it remains stable through the camera pullback.

## 04 — Routes of independence

**Timestamp / duration:** `00:17–00:23` · **6 s**  
**Historical period:** `1810–1816` *(with the Andes chronology flagged for review below)*

**On-screen text:**

> 1810–1816  
> BELGRANO · SAN MARTÍN

**Anthem:**

> OÍD EL RUIDO  
> DE ROTAS CADENAS

**Starting composition:** The Cabildo ribbon now runs over a period map of the revolutionary territories. A small two-band flag construction appears along Belgrano's route; a second route begins near the Río de la Plata and tracks west toward the Andes.

**Animation / action:** Campaign routes draw in distinct cadences: dotted planning lines first, then solid movement strokes. The western cordillera rises from topographic contours, not from a scenic cutaway. The old imperial connection is represented as a cartographic linkage made of repeating oval nodes. When the routes converge, two nodes separate cleanly and the connection breaks; the pieces become mountain contour marks. Cavalry silhouettes remain tiny scale indicators on the geography.

**Camera:** Follows the northern route, banks south-west, then skims along the Andes ridge. No heroic low-angle portrait framing.

**Color treatment:** Sky-blue routes against deep-blue contours; mountains remain mostly paper/line; colonial sand retreats to the edges.

**Gold:** A very fine inner trace appears inside the route only when coordination succeeds; it does not yet form a complete symbol.

**Transition:** The broken linkage's oval nodes flatten into the lines of handwritten script. A mountain contour straightens into the bottom edge of a declaration sheet.

**Musical energy:** **BUILDING → EPIC**, intensity 5→7.

**Suggested SFX:** Wind rising over the Andes, hoof rhythm abstracted into percussion, one dry chain/link break.

**Remotion / SVG feasibility:** All routes share an atlas coordinate system. Terrain is procedural contour polylines clipped to a mountain mask. The linkage is an SVG pattern along a path, allowing its break to be timed without simulating physical chains.

## 05 — Independence

**Timestamp / duration:** `00:23–00:28` · **5 s**  
**Historical year:** `1816`

**On-screen text:**

> 9 JUL 1816  
> INDEPENDENCIA

**Anthem:**

> VED EN TRONO  
> A LA NOBLE IGUALDAD

**Starting composition:** A declaration page occupies the foreground at a slight angle; its ruled edges extend upward and resolve into the Casa Histórica de Tucumán façade.

**Animation / action:** The building draws with measured architectural strokes. Abstract signatures accumulate on the page without reproducing a specific document. Delegation routes arrive from several directions and settle into the central doorway. On `9 JUL 1816`, a small gold seal appears—not a crown—and sends a warm hairline around the document perimeter. Equality is expressed by multiple provincial route endpoints aligning on one baseline.

**Camera:** Descends from page to doorway, then rises to a centered elevation. The motion is ceremonial and nearly symmetrical.

**Color treatment:** Bright ivory; deep-blue engraving; sky-blue regional routes. Increased negative space makes the moment feel official.

**Gold:** First unambiguous use: seal, date underline, and a narrow Sun-of-May ray behind the roofline. Maximum 6–8% of the frame.

**Transition:** The aligned route endpoints detach from the baseline and pull in competing directions. The gold perimeter remains briefly, then splits at the corners.

**Musical energy:** **EPIC → RESOLUTE**, intensity 7→6.

**Suggested SFX:** Paper settle, pen scratch, embossed seal press, restrained low bell.

**Remotion / SVG feasibility:** Building assembled from reusable architectural primitives. Signatures should be procedural bezier flourishes, not historical facsimiles. Gold reveal uses mask expansion; the route alignment is deterministic and reversible for the following fragmentation.

## 06 — A geography in dispute

**Timestamp / duration:** `00:28–00:33` · **5 s**  
**Historical period:** `1816–1853`

**On-screen text:**

> 1816–1853  
> GUERRAS CIVILES

**Anthem:**

> YA SU TRONO DIGNÍSIMO ABRIERON…

**Starting composition:** The declaration's separated route endpoints now sit over a loose historical regional mosaic. There is no modern national silhouette.

**Animation / action:** Provincial shapes do not explode; they drift a few pixels apart while directional arrows cross and overwrite one another. Unitary and federal tensions are conveyed through two line behaviors—straight radial pulls versus locally curved networks—without assigning modern party colors. Caudillo presence is suggested by sparse mounted silhouettes pinned to regions. The central gold seal loses its fill but leaves a thin ring: the unrealized center of union.

**Camera:** Lateral and slightly restless, repeatedly beginning to center before being drawn toward another province. Motion eases are sharper than elsewhere but never bouncy.

**Color treatment:** Multiple values of blue-gray and muted sand; deep-blue outlines; reduced saturation. Ivory fissures between shapes carry the conflict.

**Gold:** Only the empty seal ring; no celebratory gold.

**Transition:** Conflicting arrows bend toward the empty ring. Their intersections become the radial construction lines of a constitutional diagram.

**Musical energy:** **TENSE**, intensity 6→5.

**Suggested SFX:** Dry map folds, distant hoofbeats, low percussive cross-rhythms, pencil strokes reversing.

**Remotion / SVG feasibility:** Regional units need simplified period-specific geometry with independent transforms. Route styles are semantic props rather than literal troop maps. A shared node graph allows conflict vectors to interpolate into convergence vectors.

## 07 — Organization

**Timestamp / duration:** `00:33–00:38` · **5 s**  
**Historical period:** `1853–1880`

**On-screen text:**

> 1853  
> CONSTITUCIÓN

then

> ORGANIZACIÓN NACIONAL

**Anthem:**

> …LAS PROVINCIAS UNIDAS DEL SUD

**Starting composition:** The empty ring sits at center with the fragmented geography around it. Faint constitutional text rules and radial guide lines overlay the paper.

**Animation / action:** One by one, provincial nodes lock into a connected network. The network stabilizes before the territorial silhouette does, emphasizing institutional connection over anachronistic borders. `1853` appears at the instant the network closes. During `1853–1880`, exterior contours continue changing subtly; the final arrangement is stable but not falsely identical to today's map.

**Color treatment:** Clean sky blue returns; deep-blue type and borders; sand recedes. Paper is at its brightest since 1816.

**Gold:** The former seal ring becomes a thin gold constitutional node, then dims to a watermark.

**Transition:** Network edges extend beyond the continent. One reaches the Atlantic; matching lines appear on the far side in Europe and curve back as migration routes.

**Musical energy:** **HOPEFUL / RESOLUTE**, intensity 5→6.

**Suggested SFX:** Soft clicks as nodes settle, page turn, a sustained string/harmonic opening.

**Remotion / SVG feasibility:** Use a graph layout over period geography and animate node constraints with interpolated coordinates. Avoid fluid path morphs between unrelated country silhouettes; reveal the evolving boundary by contour replacement under a moving mask.

## 08 — The nation becomes a network

**Timestamp / duration:** `00:38–00:45` · **7 s**  
**Historical period:** `1880–1930`

**On-screen text:**

> 1880–1930  
> INMIGRACIÓN · FERROCARRIL · CIUDAD

**Anthem:**

> Y LOS LIBRES DEL MUNDO RESPONDEN…

**Starting composition:** A wide Atlantic view. Argentina is now the compositional destination, but Europe and the Mediterranean remain visible. Several fine routes wait as faint dotted arcs.

**Animation / action:** Routes depart Europe in staggered waves and converge on the Río de la Plata. Ship hull strokes elongate into railway sleepers; the route arrives at port and grows inland as a rail network. Wheat hatching appears across the pampas. Buenos Aires rises as a sparse skyline built from façades and cornices. A mate and bombilla can appear only as a tiny drafting-table vignette near a station label—cultural punctuation, not a hero object.

**Camera:** Crosses the Atlantic along the central migrant line, dives into the port, then accelerates along a rail toward the interior. Ends beside an urban rail switch.

**Color treatment:** Luminous sky blue, deep-blue machinery/architecture, pale wheat-gold only in crops. Density increases without darkening the paper.

**Gold:** Agricultural hatching and a few arrival nodes; still secondary to blue.

**Transition:** The rail switch multiplies. Sleepers tighten until they read as factory-window rows; a station clock becomes the circular face of an industrial gauge.

**Musical energy:** **BUILDING / HOPEFUL**, intensity 5→7.

**Suggested SFX:** Harbor ambience, steam release, rail rhythm synchronized to the score, distant city bell.

**Remotion / SVG feasibility:** Routes generated from cubic beziers; vehicle symbols orient from path tangents. Rail lines share the route path, adding sleepers at fixed arc-length intervals. City growth uses modular façade components and masks, not hundreds of unique drawings.

## 09 — Mass politics and interrupted institutions

**Timestamp / duration:** `00:45–00:52` · **7 s**  
**Historical period:** `1930–1976`

**On-screen text:**

> 1930–1976  
> INDUSTRIA · MULTITUDES · RUPTURAS

**Anthem:**

> ¡AL GRAN PUEBLO ARGENTINO, SALUD!

**Starting composition:** Factory-window geometry grows from the railway switch. Beyond it, the Congreso dome, Casa Rosada balcony line, smokestacks, microphones, and a broad Plaza de Mayo axis coexist as one editorial city section.

**Animation / action:** A horizontal civic timeline moves beneath the architecture. It repeatedly advances, then is interrupted by narrow vertical cuts marking coups; it resumes each time rather than becoming a partisan chronology. Workers and crowd silhouettes fill the plaza in waves. Perón and Eva may appear as two recognizable but highly simplified balcony/microphone silhouettes for under one second, integrated among larger crowds rather than treated as portraits. Industrial pistons, ballot marks, and broadcast rings share one rhythm.

**Camera:** Faster lateral tracking through a layered urban panorama. Brief push toward the balcony, immediately followed by a wider pullback to the people and institutions.

**Color treatment:** Denser deep blue, sky-blue crowd flags, neutral warm grays for concrete/industry. Still a light composition with ample ivory sky.

**Gold:** Tiny institutional ticks on the timeline; each is interrupted when the line breaks. No gold on political figures.

**Transition:** The final vertical interruption expands into a pale empty band. Crowds and buildings continue on either side, but paths entering the band stop and vanish.

**Musical energy:** **DRIVING / TENSE**, intensity 7→8→5.

**Suggested SFX:** Machinery pulse, radio static, crowd swell, microphone feedback hint, abrupt tape-stop at the final interruption.

**Remotion / SVG feasibility:** Modular city panorama in 3–4 parallax layers. Timeline interruptions are clip-path shutters. Crowd cycles should use seeded variations and low-frequency motion; the two leaders are static signature silhouettes with no facial detail.

## 10 — The atlas is wounded

**Timestamp / duration:** `00:52–00:57` · **5 s**  
**Historical period:** `1976–1983`

**On-screen text:**

> 1976–1983  
> DICTADURA · TERRORISMO DE ESTADO

**Anthem:** None.

**Starting composition:** The pale interruption band fills the frame. Only fragments of the city grid remain near the edges; the center is large, unprinted ivory-gray space.

**Animation / action:** A civic route tries to cross. Its nodes dim one at a time and some disappear, leaving empty coordinate rings. Fine parallel lines suggest controlled space and censorship without depicting instruments of violence. A restrained ring of white headscarf-like marks may orbit the Plaza de Mayo coordinate—abstract, quiet, historically specific, and subject to verification—while the main timeline becomes discontinuous.

**Camera:** Nearly static. A very slow backward drift increases the emptiness. Motion does not stop completely; the paper still breathes.

**Color treatment:** Pale gray-blue, desaturated sky blue, softened deep blue. Background remains light. Contrast is lowered, not inverted.

**Gold:** Removed entirely.

**Transition:** A surviving rectangular grid at the edge expands into pitch markings. The missing coordinate rings remain faintly visible outside the pitch, so 1978 is visibly contained within the dictatorship rather than detached from it.

**Musical energy:** **RESTRAINED / SOLEMN**, intensity 2.

**Suggested SFX:** Low room tone, distant footsteps, soft wind, short drop to near-silence as the nodes vanish.

**Remotion / SVG feasibility:** Node disappearance is opacity plus a short masked erosion, not random particle decay. Use deterministic timing and generous holds. The headscarf-ring symbol requires historical review and respectful abstraction before production.

## 12 — South Atlantic distance

**Timestamp / duration:** `01:01–01:07` · **6 s**  
**Historical year:** `1982`

**On-screen text:**

> 1982  
> GUERRA DE MALVINAS

Small cartographic label:

> ISLAS MALVINAS · SOBERANÍA DISPUTADA

**Anthem:**

> O JUREMOS  
> CON GLORIA MORIR

**Starting composition:** Continental Argentina appears at left; a great field of ivory-blue South Atlantic occupies most of the frame. The former stadium ellipse is now a wind contour crossing the sea.

**Animation / action:** The camera follows a measured southeast bearing. Coordinates tick past slowly, making distance the dominant fact. Naval and air routes appear as thin interrupted paths; none reaches its endpoint with a flourish. The islands resolve from ocean hatching. Minimal soldier silhouettes are seen from behind at the lower edge, small against sea and weather. A route stops before the lyric finishes; the held gap is the emotional center.

**Musical energy:** **SOLEMN**, intensity 3→4, with a breath before transition.

**Suggested SFX:** South Atlantic wind, low hull resonance, distant radio texture, then near-silence.

**Remotion / SVG feasibility:** Ocean is procedural parallel curves and sparse stipple; coordinates move in a stable world-space projection. Islands use a simplified verified path. Route dash gaps are art-directed, not random. Label wording must remain legible at delivery resolution.

## 13 — The unfinished laurels

**Timestamp / duration:** `01:07–01:13` · **6 s**  
**Historical year:** `1986`

**On-screen text:**

> MÉXICO · 1986  
> ARGENTINA · CAMPEÓN DEL MUNDO  
> MARADONA · 10

**Anthem:**

> SEAN ETERNOS  
> LOS LAURELES…

**Starting composition:** The ocean geometry has become an overhead football pitch. One sky-blue dot marked `10` waits left of center; several smaller deep-blue player marks establish opposition and space.

**Animation / action:** A single line accelerates from the `10`, bends through a choreographed series of spatial decisions, and reaches the goal. The dot expands into a compact Maradona motion silhouette—torso lean, low center of gravity, left-foot follow-through—then resolves into a trophy-lift pose. Gold grows along only the **left half** of a laurel ellipse. The winning line exits the trophy as a fine golden thread and embeds itself in the lower timeline, continuing invisibly toward the future. The ellipsis in the lyric is literal and important.

**Camera:** Begins orthographic, then drops into a shallow tracking angle behind the trajectory. It rises with the trophy but stops before full visual closure.

**Color treatment:** Sky blue returns strongly; deep-blue pitch construction; ivory crowd field. Gold is warmer and brighter than in 1978.

**Gold:** Goal trajectory core, trophy, and exactly one half-laurel. The incomplete right half is reserved for 2022.

**Transition:** Camera follows the golden thread away from the stadium. Pitch markings compress into annual ticks, turning the sports line into the national timeline.

**Musical energy:** **TRIUMPHANT**, intensity 8, ending on an intentionally suspended chord.

**Suggested SFX:** Footfall accents, one ball strike, stadium roar, bright metallic shimmer cut short before resolution.

**Remotion / SVG feasibility:** The movement is a designed bezier trajectory, not a literal recreation of copyrighted match footage. Player symbols orient to path tangents. Pose change uses matched anchor points between two simple silhouettes. Laurel leaves are procedural instances along an ellipse, with the right branch withheld.

## 14 — Acceleration and fracture

**Timestamp / duration:** `01:13–01:18` · **5 s**  
**Historical period:** `1990–2001`

**On-screen text:**

> 1990–2001

then, alone:

> 2001

**Anthem:** None.

**Starting composition:** The golden 1986 thread runs horizontally as a thin timeline. Urban blocks and financial ledger marks grow above and below it.

**Animation / action:** Year ticks accelerate. City traffic routes, price/ledger lines, broadcast waves, and changing industrial outlines slide past. No single policy, currency sign, president, or party becomes the shorthand. Near `2001`, several synchronized systems overrun their guides: the timeline kinks, the graph line drops out of registration, urban paths meet a closed node, and the screen acquires fine paper fissures. The gold thread survives but goes beneath the paper rather than breaking.

**Camera:** High-speed lateral track along the timeline, with a sudden deceleration and short downward jolt at `2001`.

**Color treatment:** Faster alternation between sky blue, navy, and neutral gray; colors desaturate at the fracture. Background remains ivory.

**Gold:** The buried 1986 thread is visible only in two brief gaps; no new gold.

**Transition:** One paper fissure becomes the vertical stem of a new-century timeline. The camera rotates 90° and begins moving upward through years.

**Musical energy:** **TENSE / ACCELERATING → ABRUPT RESTRAINT**, intensity 6→8→3.

**Suggested SFX:** Mechanical ticks, street/transport blur, ledger stamp, layered impacts collapsing into a dry paper crack.

**Remotion / SVG feasibility:** Timeline is a single normalized progress driver for ticks, labels, and camera. Fractures are authored SVG paths revealed by masks. Avoid data-like specificity unless later verified and narratively approved.

## 17 — Gold returns

**Timestamp / duration:** `01:27–01:30` · **3 s**  
**Historical year:** `2021`

**On-screen text:**

> 2021  
> COPA AMÉRICA  
> MESSI

**Anthem:** None.

**Starting composition:** South America is formed by the former laurel leaf. A route connects Argentina to Rio de Janeiro; the 2014 blue branch survives beneath it.

## A. Visual system

### Canvas and paper

- **Base:** `#F5F0E6`; scene-to-scene variation may move between `#F7F3EA` and `#F2EFE7` by no more than 3–4% luminance.
- **Texture:** two extremely low-opacity deterministic layers: large paper fiber and fine print grain. Texture must remain fixed in page/world space while the camera moves; otherwise it reads as video noise.
- **Working atlas:** one oversized virtual canvas (suggested 7680 × 4320 design units) containing geographic clusters connected by authored camera paths. The delivery viewport samples this atlas at 1920 × 1080.
- **Safe zones:** primary text stays within 8% horizontal and 7% vertical safe margins, except deliberately cropped cartographic labels.

### Palette roles

| Role | Primary values | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#F5F0E6`, `#F7F3EA`, `#F2EFE7` | Never pure white; never visibly aged or stained. |
| Sky blue | `#75B6D9`, `#8BC4E0`, `#A5D3E8` | Routes, living geography, connection, flags, football movement. |
| Deep blue | `#174A73`, `#245B82` | Type, coastlines, silhouettes, structural linework. Keep below roughly 25% area coverage. |
| Gold | `#C49A45`, `#D2AE61` | Earned historical meaning only; matte, never neon or glowing. |
| Restrained gray-blue | proposed `#A9B8BE`, `#CCD3D1` | Dictatorship, loss, interrupted systems, not a dark-mode palette. |

### Maps

- Use simplified, authored SVG geography with enough recognizable coastal and provincial character to orient the viewer.
- Maintain one geographic coordinate convention and projection per world-scale sequence. Projection changes must occur visibly through grid morphs, never silently.
- Historical layers are separate geometry sets: 1776 viceroyalty, revolutionary-era regions, 1816–1853 provincial configuration, 1853–1880 organization, modern mainland, islands, and Antarctic claim.
- Modern national maps may use the bicontinental concept; historical maps must never inherit today's national border by convenience.
- Map labels use letterspacing and follow local geography only when legibility remains high.

### Typography

- **Display serif:** dates and anthem lines; high contrast, editorial, uppercase/small caps. Candidate category: Cormorant/Libre Baskerville-like, ultimately bundled locally and license-verified.
- **Sans-serif:** coordinates, legends, event labels; neutral humanist face with excellent Spanish diacritics.
- **Scale at 1080p:** dates 96–156 px; event lines 28–40 px; anthem 40–66 px; map labels 16–24 px; legal/cartographic notes never below a tested delivery-safe size.
- Dates are visual anchors. Event names stay short. Anthem phrases use wider leading and enter only at emotionally earned beats.
- No paragraph text, floating caption panels, drop shadows, bevels, or glowing type.

### Lines and routes

- Base cartographic line: 1–1.5 px at 1080p; primary route: 2.5–4 px; national/key trajectory: 4–6 px.
- Rounded route caps, restrained joins, constant apparent screen weight through zoom compensation.
- Route taxonomy: dotted = planned/possible; solid sky blue = active connection; interrupted = broken/lost; double strand = memory across eras; gold core = achievement earned.
- Every moving line has a semantic origin and destination. Decorative free-floating strokes are prohibited.

### Oceans

- Represent water with widely spaced bathymetric curves, isobars, coordinate ticks, and sparse directional hatching.
- The ocean should feel like paper carrying information, not a filled blue field.
- Sound and line speed distinguish Atlantic exploration, migration, and Malvinas; the visual vocabulary remains related.

### Borders and disputed territories

- Administrative boundary: fine deep-blue dotted or hairline.
- Historical uncertainty/change: layered or interrupted contour, never a falsely crisp modern outline.
- Sovereignty claim/dispute: distinct dash/hatch plus explicit minimal label; do not communicate legal status through color alone.
- Claimed Antarctic sector: sector rays and hatch with a Treaty note; visually included in the bicontinental composition without presenting the claim as internationally settled.

### Silhouettes

- 8–30 SVG paths each, with no facial features. Recognition comes from posture, clothing contour, prop, scale, and context.
- Three detail levels: map icon, midground figure, hero motion silhouette.
- Crowds use a curated reusable library with seeded spacing and mirroring; no random motion on every figure.
- Political and military figures never receive gold outlines.

### Historical buildings

- Construct with reusable primitives: base, columns, arches, pediments, cornices, windows, domes, balcony, clock.
- Draw on in architectural order (axis → mass → openings → detail), as though the atlas is drafting them.
- Keep perspective shallow and editorial; avoid photoreal texture.

### Football scenes

- Pitch geometry emerges from existing grids and returns to geography afterward.
- Players are nodes/silhouettes; match meaning comes from trajectory and spacing, not literal replay.
- Stadium crowds are patterned strokes. Trophy is a simplified original vector interpretation.
- The number `10`, one continuous trajectory, and the incomplete/completed laurel create the 1986→2022 memory system.

### Timelines

- Timelines are geographic routes viewed edge-on. They can rotate into borders, pitch lines, railways, or vertical year axes.
- Major ticks: year labels; minor ticks: short rules only. Never show every year if it creates noise.
- Institutional interruptions are gaps/cuts in the timeline; historical time itself continues beneath them.

### Sun of May

- Use a reduced geometric symbol: alternating straight/flame rays, no detailed face except possibly a minimal central disk mark.
- Appearances: faint architectural hint in 1816; watermark in organization; one complete expansion at the final `LIBERTAD`.
- Keep it below 15% frame width until the finale.

### Gold discipline

- 1492–1810: orientation/glint only.
- 1816: first earned seal and ray.
- 1816–1976: institutional/agricultural accents, never blanket decoration.
- 1976–1983: removed, except the contextually bounded 1978 trophy.
- 1986: strong but incomplete.
- 2014: distant/incomplete.
- 2021: returning.
- 2022: completed laurels and trophy.
- Finale: strongest and final use on `LIBERTAD`.

## C. Key transitions

### 1492 → 1776 — route becomes administration

The first Atlantic route is not merely a travel line. As the camera reaches the Río de la Plata, its secondary branches settle against rivers, coasts, and administrative contours. The ocean-facing curve remains fluid while inland branches become a measured boundary system. `1492` stays behind as a small atlas annotation; `1776` grows only when the map has acquired administrative structure. This communicates a shift from contact/movement to colonial organization without pretending that Argentina already exists.

### 1808 → 1810 — broken connection becomes architecture

The Spain–Río de la Plata link loses ink in its middle. The two remaining ends rotate and lengthen into the vertical sides of the Cabildo; longitude ticks become windows; a route node becomes the clock/central axis. The absence in the imperial connection literally supplies the negative space of the Plaza de Mayo. This is the first transition where a geopolitical break creates a new civic form.

### 1810 → 1816 — civic pulse becomes campaign, then signature

The crowd's sky-blue pulse exits as the flag ribbon, enters a period map, and separates into campaign routes. The western route climbs into Andes contours; the symbolic linkage breaks. Its oval nodes flatten and concatenate into cursive-like declaration strokes. A terrain contour straightens into the page baseline, while route endpoints from multiple regions arrive as aligned delegation marks. The flow is: **voice → route → break → written sovereignty**.

### Civil wars → national organization — competing vectors become a network

During civil conflict, arrows overwrite one another and provincial units drift apart. Their crossing points remain on screen as potential network nodes. Instead of forcing the shapes together, the animation first bends each conflict vector toward shared nodes. Connections stabilize; only then do geographic pieces approach a coherent arrangement. This distinction is essential: political organization is represented as negotiated connectivity, not as pieces magnetically snapping into a timeless border.

### Immigration → industrial Argentina — ship becomes rail becomes factory rhythm

A ship route enters the port and its dashed wake regularizes into railway sleepers. The camera travels the same line inland. As the rail passes a switch, the sleepers rotate 90° and tighten into factory windows; the station clock becomes an industrial gauge; steam becomes broadcast rings. Migrant crowd marks remain and multiply into urban/mass-political crowds, preserving people—not machinery—as the human continuity.

### 1976 → 1978 — wounded grid becomes bounded pitch

The dictatorship scene never clears. One surviving rectangular fragment expands into pitch markings, but missing nodes remain outside the touchlines and the palette only saturates inside the stadium. The audience may experience the sport's genuine public emotion while the overhead pullback reveals its historical enclosure. This avoids both erasing the victory and isolating it from state-terror context.

### 1978 → Malvinas — stadium ellipse becomes South Atlantic weather

The stadium bowl elongates into an isobar; crowd strokes align into wind; the trophy axis turns into a longitude. The camera pulls out far enough to recover mainland geography, then follows that longitude southeast. Stadium sound loses high frequencies until it becomes wind. Gold is left behind at the stadium rather than carried to the islands.

### Malvinas → 1986 — interrupted route becomes possibility

The 1982 naval/air route stops in open ocean. Its end curls into a neutral circle, not a victory symbol. A second circle and two perpendicular ocean parallels gradually establish the center circle and halfway line of a pitch. Cold wind becomes stadium air, while color warms slowly. The same interrupted path is re-energized as an athletic trajectory without implying that football redeems or resolves the war.

### 1986 → 2014 — triumph becomes a buried memory thread

The 1986 goal/trophy line exits the laurel and compresses into a horizontal timeline. It continues beneath the 1990s and even passes under the 2001 paper fissure; it is never erased. The new-century timeline rotates vertical, and Messi's sky-blue `10` path coils around it. At 2014 both lines occupy nearly the same coordinate but remain separated by a few pixels/depth layers. The missing right laurel begins in blue, then stops.

### 2014 → 2021 — an incomplete leaf becomes a continent

One unfinished laurel leaf enlarges until its serrated shape simplifies into South America. The leaf stem becomes a route from Argentina to Rio de Janeiro. On completion, only several leaves turn gold: 2021 is a necessary breakthrough, not the final completion. A team-circle replaces the solitary `10`, changing the visual grammar from individual burden to collective achievement.

### 2021 → 2022 — parallel lines become a double strand

The resurfaced 1986 gold line and the 2021 sky-blue line run beside one another from South America toward Qatar. They twist once like a restrained braid, preserving both eras, then enter the pitch separately. They merge only at the decisive 2022 action. The brief backward camera travel reveals the 36-year span before slinging forward to the completed laurels.

### 2022 → contemporary Argentina — laurels become infrastructure

Completed laurel leaves detach in a controlled sequence. Their center veins straighten into routes; leaf contours become agricultural rows, scientific orbit marks, rails, urban streets, and ocean lines. The trophy is not carried into the contemporary scene. Achievement disperses into the country as living connection, shifting the protagonist from football figures back to Argentina.

### Contemporary Argentina → LIBERTAD finale — network becomes geography

The contemporary network calls back named route layers from across the master atlas. Each returns in its own motion signature but converges toward a modern map: maritime arcs form eastern curvature, Andes contours form the west, rail/civic lines build the interior, the Malvinas bearing establishes the island inset/relationship, and southern sector rays extend toward Antarctica. Complexity is then removed in three typographic impacts. `LIBERTAD` does not sit over a busy collage; each recurrence strips away information until only nation, dates, Sun, and paper remain.

## D. Maradona → Messi visual motif alternatives

### Alternative 1 — The golden memory line + incomplete laurel **(recommended)**

**1986:** The iconic movement is abstracted into one route. It becomes gold at the goal, rises through Maradona's trophy silhouette, draws only the left laurel branch, and escapes as a thin thread beneath the national timeline. `SEAN ETERNOS LOS LAURELES…` remains typographically and graphically open.

**2014:** Messi's blue trajectory approaches the buried thread. The right laurel attempts to grow in pale blue, then stops.

**2021:** Several leaves on that branch gain gold; the two trajectories become parallel.

**2022:** The lines merge, the camera exposes `36 AÑOS`, the right branch completes, and `…QUE SUPIMOS CONSEGUIR` resolves the suspended lyric.

**Why it is strongest:** It binds lyric, chronology, geography, football, and the overall one-line concept in a single device. It also survives small-screen viewing and is highly controllable with SVG path animation. It honors both players without requiring likeness or literal match replay.

### Alternative 2 — Trophy-pose skeletal morph

**1986:** A simplified Maradona trophy-lift silhouette is built around seven anchor points: feet, hips, shoulders, hands, trophy center. The pose freezes as a faint atlas constellation.

**2014/2021:** The constellation drifts through the timeline; some anchor points are approached but not occupied by the Messi figure.

**2022:** The same anchors realign into Messi's trophy pose and surrounding team geometry. The negative space between raised arms becomes a laurel crown, completing the lyric.

**Strength:** Immediate human/emotional recognition and a powerful match shape.

**Risk:** More dependent on accurate, respectful pose design; can feel like a logo morph or imply interchangeability between distinct people. It is also more vulnerable to resemblance/copyright concerns if over-detailed.

### Alternative 3 — The number 10 across a 36-year coordinate field

**1986:** The `10` is plotted as two cartographic forms: `1` is a longitude/route; `0` is the center circle/trophy halo. The zero opens at one point, leaving the numeral geometrically unfinished.

**1990–2021:** The open `0` becomes a timeline aperture through which key years pass. Messi's `10` arrives from the opposite side, rotating the coordinate field.

**2022:** The two open circular arcs close into a complete `0`, then expand into a World Cup/trophy halo and Sun-of-May disk. Thirty-six small ticks around the circle reveal the elapsed years.

**Strength:** Extremely minimal, elegant, legible, and culturally clear.

**Risk:** It privileges the shared shirt number over team/national history and can feel brand-like. Thirty-six ticks may become decorative noise.

### Alternative 4 — Two hemispheres of one world

**1986:** Mexico and Argentina are joined by a gold geodesic arc that draws half of a globe/laurel.

**2022:** A second geodesic from Argentina to Qatar completes the opposite hemisphere. The two arcs rotate into the World Cup silhouette, then flatten into the bicontinental map grid.

**Strength:** Most cartographic and globally scaled; transitions cleanly into the final map.

**Risk:** Less emotionally specific to Maradona and Messi; the lyric/laurel relationship is more indirect.

### Recommendation

Choose **Alternative 1** as the primary motif and borrow only the shared anchor logic from Alternative 2 for the trophy poses. This hybrid keeps the film's true protagonist—the traveling line—while giving the 2022 climax a human silhouette. Avoid combining the number-10 circle and world-hemisphere concepts as equal motifs; too many simultaneous symbols would weaken the clean payoff.

## F. Historical and cartographic verification checklist

These are production gates, not silent changes to the agreed narrative.

### Required chronology checks

- **1492 framing:** Verify that visuals communicate the opening of sustained transatlantic contact from a European perspective, not the date of European arrival in present-day Argentina. Any local Río de la Plata event markers require their own dates.
- **Viceroyalty:** Verify the 1776 creation date, the intended snapshot year, and the approximate administrative extent. Boundaries changed; do not treat one polygon as stable through the whole period.
- **British invasions:** Distinguish 1806 and 1807 if two approach/reversal pulses are shown. Confirm Buenos Aires/Montevideo geography used in the routes.
- **1808 crisis:** Confirm that the Napoleon silhouette/arrows refer to the invasion/abdications and Spanish monarchical crisis without compressing all European events into one false instant.
- **May Revolution:** Verify Cabildo architecture appropriate to 1810 rather than its modern altered form, plus period flags, umbrellas, and plaza proportions if shown.
- **Belgrano / flag:** Verify the date and location implied by the flag sequence and avoid presenting the modern flag specification as fully settled at its first creation.
- **San Martín / Andes:** **Narrative adjustment to approve:** the Crossing of the Andes is primarily January–February **1817**, after the 9 July 1816 declaration. The requested `1810–1816` section may show preparation and westward movement toward the Andes, but a completed crossing to Chile should either (a) occur immediately after the declaration with a visible `1817` tick, or (b) rename the campaign block `1810–1817`. The storyboard currently stops the pre-1816 route at the ridge so it does not silently misdate the crossing.
- **Independence:** Verify Casa de Tucumán's 1816 appearance; a depiction based on the later reconstructed façade should be identified/stylized carefully. Verify document/signature abstraction does not reproduce an anachronistic design.
- **Civil wars:** Review the `1816–1853` regional geometry and caudillo indicators with a historian. Avoid a binary map implying stable, uniform Unitarian/Federal territorial camps throughout the entire period.
- **Constitution / organization:** Confirm how to visualize 1853 while Buenos Aires was initially outside the Argentine Confederation, and how to represent 1860/1861 settlement and 1880 federalization. The network-first approach is designed to avoid falsely instantaneous unity.
- **Territorial consolidation:** Verify frontier and indigenous-territory representation for 1853–1880. A stabilizing national state must not be visualized as uncontested occupation of the entire present-day territory.

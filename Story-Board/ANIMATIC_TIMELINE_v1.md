## 1. Editing principle

The soundtrack is now the master clock. The historical structure and
visual language of the approved director's storyboard are preserved, but
scene boundaries may move to land on musical changes.

The film remains one continuous atlas. The memory line is never
recreated as an unrelated object: it persists across scenes and changes
semantic role.

Hard cuts remain exceptional. The main language is path-follow, map
morph, match-shape, camera continuation, and shared geometry.

The ending deliberately uses the near-silence after approximately
01:42.2 as negative space.

## 4. Camera and memory-line continuity

### Frames 0--335

Camera follows the Atlantic route. Memory line = maritime/colonial
connection.

### Frames 336--809

Camera closes toward Río de la Plata and revolutionary civic geometry.
Memory line = pulse → campaign route → declaration stroke.

### Frames 810--1361

The line splits, negotiates conflict, then regularizes into national
network, railway and migration routes.

### Frames 1362--1721

Camera speed increases through a layered city panorama. Memory line =
civic timeline repeatedly interrupted by institutional breaks and
political violence.

### Frames 1722--1907

Movement becomes more controlled and spatially restricted. Memory line
becomes discontinuous. Do not clear the atlas and do not introduce black
backgrounds.

### Frames 1908--2084

A surviving rectangle becomes the football pitch. The wounded historical
layer remains outside it.

### Frames 2085--2258

Pitch ellipse stretches into South Atlantic cartography. The route
toward Malvinas interrupts in open ocean. During the 1983 beat, a civic
line reconnects without becoming celebratory spectacle.

### Frames 2259--2444

The interrupted path becomes athletic possibility. Maradona's route
earns gold and draws only the left laurel branch.

### Frames 2445--2699

The gold line compresses into a buried horizontal thread and survives
the 2001 fissure. A new sky-blue `10` path rises around the new-century
timeline.

### Frames 2700--2891

2014 nearly connects both eras. 2021 restores several gold leaves and
places the two trajectories in parallel.

### Frames 2892--3065

Both strands travel toward Qatar, merge at the decisive 2022 beat,
briefly reveal `1986 → 2022 · 36 AÑOS`, then complete the right laurel.

### Frames 3066--3149

Laurel/infrastructure/network geometry simplifies into Argentina. Remove
information rather than adding spectacle. End on paper, geography, date
and silence.

## 5. Final 1986 → 2022 motif lock

**1986:** Maradona route turns gold at the decisive action. Left laurel
only.\
**2014:** Messi trajectory approaches the buried gold thread but does
not meet it. Right branch begins in pale blue and stops.\
**2021:** Several right-branch leaves turn gold. Gold and blue
trajectories run beside one another.\
**2022:** Lines merge. `1986 → 2022 · 36 AÑOS` appears during a brief
backward camera reveal. Right laurel completes. The suspended anthem
phrase resolves with `…QUE SUPIMOS CONSEGUIR`.

Do not add the number-10-circle or globe motifs as competing master
metaphors.

## 6. Political-period visual lock

For 1930--1983, use chronology and concrete actors/events rather than a
partisan moral thesis.

-   Perón and Eva: brief simplified silhouettes inside a broader
    mass-politics composition.
-   Armed revolutionary organizations: represent explicitly as part of
    political radicalization/armed violence, not as generic peaceful
    crowds.
-   Para-state violence and institutional deterioration: represented
    through interruption, fragmentation and competing routes.
-   1976: military takeover transitions into dictatorship/state
    repression; do not make the date a heroic centerpiece.
-   Dictatorship: visible copy is `1976–1983 · DICTADURA` only.
    **Prohibido:** the phrase `TERRORISMO DE ESTADO` must never appear
    on screen or in any visible copy (editorial lock: it invites
    biased political readings). No white-headscarf motif.
-   1978: sporting achievement can be emotionally real while the
    surrounding wounded atlas remains visible.
-   1982: solemn South Atlantic treatment; no triumphalism.
-   1983: short civic reconnection, `1983 · DEMOCRACIA`; no individual
    political leader as protagonist.

## 8. Remotion implementation contract

Before building the full film, implement a benchmark segment using the
same production components that will survive into the final composition.

Recommended benchmark: **00:57.4--01:12.4 (frames 1722--2171)**.

Why this segment: - tests the light-but-somber palette; - tests wounded
atlas continuity; - tests dictatorship → 1978 → South Atlantic
transition; - contains both restrained and active camera language; -
forces the memory line to change semantic roles without a hard reset; -
is difficult enough that, if it works, the rest of the visual system is
credible.

Required shared components for the benchmark: `AtlasCanvas`,
`CameraPath`, `CartographicGrid`, `HistoricalDate`, `EventLabel`,
`MemoryLine/RouteLine`, `FootballPitch`, `TerritoryHighlight`,
`PaperTexture`, `SceneBridge`.

Do not build bespoke one-off scene systems where a shared path/component
can express the same transition.
